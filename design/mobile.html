The problem
Walk-up kiosks at the counter were slow. People queued, staff repeated themselves, and the menu was hard to scan in a rush.
The approach
We started by listening. Two weeks of sitting at the counter, watching where people hesitated. The result is a voice-first flow that confirms out loud and never hides the price.
The outcome
Piloted across three outlets. Checkout got measurably faster and staff stopped being the bottleneck at peak hours.

CONFIRM ORDER
The problem
The storefront was tied to a monolithic WordPress theme. Every product change meant a deploy, and pages took over a second to paint.
The approach
We split the content layer from the front end. WordPress stayed as the editor experience, GraphQL served the data, and the storefront became a static-first Next.js app on the edge.
The outcome
Time to first byte dropped from 1.2 seconds to under 200 milliseconds. Editors ship changes without touching code.
