Currently taking on freelance work and open to full-time roles. Usually reply within a day.
I got into this because I was curious why some websites feel easy to use and others feel like homework. That curiosity turned into a career.
I design interfaces, build WordPress systems, and plug in AI where it actually helps. I work with founders, agencies and product teams who want something clean and fast. Not trendy for the sake of it.
Off the clock I am usually reading about typography or poking at some new tool. I care about the small details that make software feel like someone actually thought about the person using it.
